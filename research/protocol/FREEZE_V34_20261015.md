## Findings the reader makes visible (stated before reading; not decisions)

1. **consul's log channel is dead for this reader.** The Hotel parser keeps records of three layouts (zerolog JSON, zerolog console, Go log `YYYY/MM/DD HH:MM:SS`); consul 1.13's documented default lines
   (`<ISO 8601> [INFO]  agent: ...`, `==> ...`) match none (synthetic-line test; not real consul output). consul therefore has docker-stats and cgroup sensors (metric channel) but **no log sensor**, although
   the declaration lists logs among consul's collected channels. The `v34` block reports the consul log lines and log sensors per epoch and says so loudly. Adding a layout would change what may be measured
   (a **preregistration decision, the user's**); not done.
2. **HO-L2 is likely to read MISS.** The prediction says consul has no own sensor in the lanes block ("writes no log in the harness"); consul's docker-stats cpu is collected and armed, and a paused
   container's cpu falls to 0, which fires in the by-construction field (BC flag). The scorer reports that as `miss (by-construction field only; counted field silent ...)`, not as a counted detection.
3. **P2 and P4 'k of 4' verdicts read `—`** at 5 episodes per (regime, rate) (`EPISODES_HOTEL` 4 not rebound, as for F and the TeaStore off-path epoch); P1 is declared for search-pause only.
4. consul is on no route and has no spans, so v2.4's exposure rule may list it among the never-exposed services although it is the target of its own regime; the block flags that as the rule's.
5. HO-C is true by construction of the lane rules (Hotel declares no coordinator and no lag lane); the scorer says `hit (by construction)`.
6. Calibration is the epoch's own no-fault cells only (5 per rate, 10 cells), no legacy quiet cells (finding B1); the leave-one-cell-out calibration is therefore coarse.
